How It Works

🎃

1. Pick a festival

Choose from Halloween, Christmas, Diwali, Eid, Lunar New Year, Valentine's Day, or Birthday. Each festival has its own vessel, palette, and fortunes. Festivals open early (7–14 days before the official date) and stay open after (same grace period).

🍪

2. Hold to crack

Press and hold the cookie vessel for 2 seconds. Watch it shake, burst particles, and reveal your fortune. One cookie per day — no rerolls, no catch-up.

🔮

3. Read your fortune

Each fortune is crafted to pass the "screenshot test" — something you'd actually send to a friend. Rarities range from Common to Legendary; the rarer the fortune, the deeper it cuts.

📤

4. Share it

Send your fortune to Twitter/X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, or LinkedIn. Social platforms show a preview card with your fortune image. Or download the PNG and save it.

What You Can Do

🎯 One cookie per day — each festival can be cracked once per calendar day.
🌑 Dark / light mode — toggle the theme; your preference is saved.
📓 Fortune journal — every crack is saved with date, festival, and rarity. Filter by festival or clear anytime.
🎂 Age-aware birthdays — set your birth year in Settings; birthday fortunes match your age band.
📱 Social sharing — share to 5 networks, copy a link, or download the PNG card.
🎯 Early & late windows — festivals open 7–14 days early and close 7–14 days late, so you never miss the moment.
🔒 No account needed — everything stays in your browser. No login, no tracking.
